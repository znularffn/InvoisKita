Install InvoisKita App

Use full-screen on iPhone & Android

Your device supports instant 1-tap app installation!

iPhone / iPad (iOS) Open in Safari
  1. Open this app link in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share icon ↑ at the bottom toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap Add — the InvoisKita icon will appear on your iPhone Home Screen!
Android Open in Chrome / Edge
  1. Open this app link in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the Menu icon ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap "Install App" or "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Confirm Install to launch it like a native Android app!

Tip: Host this single HTML file on any HTTPS link (such as Netlify Drop or GitHub Pages) so iOS Safari and Android Chrome enable full offline & home-screen app installation.
